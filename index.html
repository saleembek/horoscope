How to read this

Sun is your core self. Moon is your emotions and what makes you feel safe. Rising (Ascendant) is the sign that was rising on the eastern horizon when you were born. It shapes your first impression and outward style.

Houses are the 12 areas of life (money, home, work, love and so on). This page uses whole-sign houses: your Rising sign is your 1st house, the next sign is your 2nd, and so on.

Transits are today's planets making angles to your birth planets:

☌ Conjunction 0° merging, emphasis
⚹ Sextile 60° opportunity
□ Square 90° tension, push to act
△ Trine 120° easy flow
☍ Opposition 180° balance, other people

℞ Retrograde means a planet looks like it's moving backward from Earth. Read it as a time to review and redo, not to launch.

Theme of the year uses annual profections, an old technique. Each birthday moves your focus one house forward, and the planet that rules that house becomes your "year planet."

Eclipses are extra-strong New and Full Moons that happen near the Moon's nodes. They often mark turning points in the house where they fall.

How the scores work

Each day the page checks every planet against every point in your chart. Easy angles add points and hard angles take points away. Venus and Jupiter count as helpful, and Mars and Saturn as challenging. Each life area only counts the planets and houses that rule it (for example, Money = Venus, Jupiter, and your 2nd and 8th houses). The result is scaled from 1 to 5. "Best hours" follows the Moon, which moves about 1° every 2 hours, as it lines up with your chart.

Under the hood (IT notes)

Everything runs on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere, and your details are saved only in this browser.

  • Birth time is converted to UTC, then to a Julian Day number, the continuous day count astronomers use.
  • Sun & Moon use low-precision formulas from Jean Meeus's Astronomical Algorithms (Sun within ~0.01°, Moon within ~1°, so Moon phase times are accurate to about ±2 hours).
  • The planets use NASA JPL Keplerian orbital elements. The code solves Kepler's equation, then subtracts Earth's position to get the view from Earth.
  • Event dates come from scanning the sky one day at a time. New and Full Moon times are then narrowed down by bisection.
  • The zodiac is tropical (Western astrology), with signs every 30° starting at 0° Aries.

For reflection and fun. The astronomy is real; how you read it is up to you.
